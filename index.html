Frequently asked questions

Is the Dominican Republic E-Ticket free?

Yes. The official government E-Ticket form is free. Our business may charge a separate, optional fee for assistance, which must be clearly disclosed before payment.

Are you the Dominican Republic government?

No. Dominican E-Ticket Assistance is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Dominican government or its agencies.

Will you issue my official QR code?

No. The official confirmation is generated through the government's process. We can provide guidance and help you understand how to complete the official steps.

Can I complete the form myself?

Yes. Travelers can use the official government portal free of charge at eticket.migracion.gob.do.

How do I pay for optional assistance?

Our optional assistance service costs $15 USD per traveler. If you choose to purchase assistance, Stripe will show the total based on the number of travelers before you submit payment. The government E-Ticket itself remains free.
